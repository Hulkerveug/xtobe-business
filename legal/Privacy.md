# Privacy Policy — XTOBE BUSINESS
**Version 1.0 — Effective 2026-09-22 — privacy@xtobe.ae**

## 1. Who we are
XTOBE BUSINESS ("XTOBE", "we") operates a B2B SaaS platform for UAE clinics. For personal data processed through the platform, the **clinic is the Data Controller** and XTOBE is the **Data Processor** (see `legal/DPA.md`). This policy covers data we process as Controller (our website, accounts, billing) and explains platform processing.

## 2. Data we process (as Controller)
- **Account data:** clinic name, admin username, contact email, billing details.
- **Usage data:** login events, audit logs (action, timestamp, actor), rate-limit counters.
- **Legal basis:** contract performance (PDPL Art. 6); legitimate interest for security logging.

## 3. Platform data (as Processor, on clinic instructions)
Client contact data, masked phone numbers, chat content, appointments, leads. We never sell it, never use it for our own marketing, and never train third-party AI models on it.

## 4. Retention
- Account/audit data: duration of subscription + 1 year.
- Platform data: deleted or anonymised within 90 days after termination (30-day export window).

## 5. Your rights (PDPL 45/2021)
Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection. Clinic staff: contact your clinic admin; end clients: contact the clinic, or email privacy@xtobe.ae and we will route the request. We respond within 30 days.

## 6. Security
TLS in transit; secrets server-side only; session auth with brute-force lockout; webhook signature verification; rate limiting; least-privilege access.

## 7. Sharing
Only with sub-processors needed to run the service: hosting (Render), Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API), payment provider. No other disclosure unless legally compelled.

## 8. Cookies
Essential session cookie only (`xtobe_session`, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict). No advertising or tracking cookies.

## 9. Contact
privacy@xtobe.ae — XTOBE BUSINESS, UAE. Complaints: UAE Data Office / u.ae.
