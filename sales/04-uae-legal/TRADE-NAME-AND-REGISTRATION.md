# UAE Trade Name & Registration — Xtobe Business

## Can we register "XTOBE BUSINESS"?

**Most likely YES — with the legal form suffix.**

### Trade name rules (federal, all emirates)
1. Must include the legal form abbreviation (LLC, FZ-LLC, etc.)
2. No improper/religiously offensive words
3. Must match the business activity
4. No Allah's names, government names, third-party names/logos
5. Must be available (checked against the federal trade-names database)
6. "Dubai", "UAE", "Emirates" need special authorization — avoid in the name

**"XTOBE BUSINESS FZ-LLC"** — clean word, no restricted terms, matches activity (business services/software). Risk: LOW.

### Recommendation for Xtobe
- **Now (pilot stage):** SHAMS — AED 5,750/year, no office needed, run from home. Register "XTOBE BUSINESS FZ-LLC" or "XTOBE TECHNOLOGY FZ-LLC".
- **Later (scale):** Move/upgrade to IFZA or Meydan for a Dubai address when clinics ask for one.
- Name reservation via Basher (basher.sa... no — **basher.ae**) — license in 15 minutes online, or via SHAMS portal.

### Registration steps (free zone route)
1. Choose free zone (SHAMS recommended)
2. Submit name + activity (Software Development / Digital Media / E-commerce)
3. Passport copy + application
4. Pay fee → license in 1–3 days
5. Open bank account (WIO / Mashreq NeoBiz — digital, free-zone friendly)
6. VAT registration only when revenue > AED 375,000/year
