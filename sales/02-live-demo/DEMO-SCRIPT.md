# Live Demo Script — 10 minutes, close the clinic

Run this on your laptop at localhost:3000. Rehearse once. The demo sells itself.

## Before the demo (5 min setup)
1. Server running: `cd xtobe-2 && node server/index.js`
2. Open tabs ready: dashboard (localhost:3000), pricing (localhost:3000/pricing.html)
3. Seed data: 2-3 conversations visible, 1 lead in pipeline, 1 AI video in preview
4. Your phone nearby for the WhatsApp moment

---

## Minute 0–2 — THE HOOK (dashboard)
> "Show me how clients message you today." (let them talk — they'll say WhatsApp + Instagram + missed messages)

Open dashboard:
- "This is every client message from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook — one screen."
- Point at a conversation: "Your receptionist answers here. Easier than WhatsApp itself."
- Click a chat, type a reply, send. "Done. Three seconds."

## Minute 2–4 — THE PAIN KILLER (bookings)
- Open appointments view: "Client messages at 10pm — AI offers your slots, books it, sends confirmation."
- "Reminder goes out the day before. No-shows drop 25–40%. For a clinic doing 200 appointments, that's AED 9,000–15,000 back in the chair every month."

## Minute 4–7 — THE WOW (AI Video Creator) ⭐
- Open studio: "Now watch this. Upload one old promo video."
- Upload → type offer "Hydrafacial AED 499 this week" → Generate.
- While it processes: "The face stays exactly the same — face lock. Only the offer changes. English and Arabic captions, hashtags included."
- Show preview: "This would take your agency 4 days and AED 500. This took 2 minutes."
- THE LINE: "One patient from this video = AED 400. The video = AED 49. You do the math."

## Minute 7–9 — THE SAFETY (approval gate)
- "Notice — nothing sent yet. Your staff approves everything. AI drafts, humans decide."
- Show the approve button. Tap it. "Now it's ready. Your team is always in control. Client numbers always masked. Data exports to Excel anytime — it's yours, not mine."

## Minute 9–10 — THE CLOSE (pricing)
- Open pricing page. Hand them the mouse. Let them scroll.
- "Elite is everything you saw — AED 1,999/month. A content creator alone costs 6,000."
- "But — first 3 clinics get the pilot: free setup, AED 299 first month. You see your real dashboard with your real messages before paying a dirham more."
- SILENCE. Wait. They'll ask questions — answer with the FAQ answers.

## Objections (30 seconds each)
| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "We use Fresha" | "Keep it. We catch the WhatsApp and Instagram leads Fresha never sees, and add the content engine." |
| "Too expensive" | "One saved appointment per week pays the month. You lose more to no-shows every week." |
| "AI sounds impersonal" | "AI drafts, your staff approves. Clients always get a human-approved message." |
| "Our clients only use WhatsApp" | "Perfect — that's where 80% of your leads are. We make sure none get missed." |
| "We have a girl for social media" | "She becomes 10x faster. She approves and posts in minutes, not days." |

## Closing line
> "Pilot is free setup, 299 for the first month. If your dashboard doesn't show more booked appointments in 30 days, cancel — no lock-in. Shall I start your WhatsApp connection today?"

## After they say yes
1. Get their WhatsApp Business number
2. Start Meta verification (48h)
3. Book the staff training call
4. Send invoice: pilot AED 299 (setup FREE)
