## Rules for outreach (what works in Dubai)

1. **Voice note first** — Dubai clinic owners reply to voice, not walls of text
2. **Timing:** Tue–Thu, 11am–1pm or 8:30–9:30pm (after maghrib, before sleep-scroll)
3. **Never say "AI" in the first message** — say "system" or "screen". AI comes in the demo
4. **Lead with the pain** (lost night bookings), not the tech
5. **Pilot scarcity is real** — 2 spots, then it's full price setup
6. **WhatsApp status:** post the site link + "2 pilot spots — beauty clinics" once a day
7. **After DEMO reply:** send https://xtobe-business.onrender.com/studio + offer 15-min call
